# Experiment 1 -- the warm-up: the ten-turn spin against the vantage warm-up (2026-11-12)

**Setting.** 39 single-storey MP3D val episodes in 8 scenes (`mp3d/sets/warmup_study_v1.json`), ground-truth
perception, `qwen2.5:14b-instruct` in both LLM roles at temperature 0 and seed 17. The `spin` arm is the running
campaign's own rows (`runs/gt_oracle_mp3d_2195ep_14b_20261111T095333Z`, commit `a77c946c`); every vantage arm ran
from a frozen snapshot of this branch on the CPU renderer (Mesa llvmpipe), with the campaign's LLM answer store
replayed and its own new answers recorded. Every warm-up action counts against the episode's 500.

**What was built.** `RPTSettings.warmup.mode == "vantage"` (`core/planning/object_search/exploration/vantage_warmup.py`
over the greedy look planner `greedy_look.py`): within `budget_steps` actions, at every action the greedy choice
between a sweep of k turns here (valued at the best k) and a walk to a reachable lattice point within 4 m (valued
by the better of one frame at its best heading or the whole circle), by **expected useful area per action** --
MapEx's observed-map viewpoint score with this camera's sensor model (`core/planning/exploration/viewpoint_gain.py`:
the 79-degree cone, rays to the detection range -- 5 m under ground truth -- with a survival prior
exp(-d / 2 m) on crossed unknown, polar-area integration, the near floor under the horizon treated as seen) and the
cost in the benchmark's actions (`viewpoint_candidates.py`: forward steps along the geodesic, the turn that faces
the way, the route's bends, the turns the look takes). It ends when no option clears 0.25 m2/action; a spot whose
circle the frames covered is a completed scan for the room ledger, as the spin's spot is.

**Arms.** `p<max walks>_b<budget>`: `p0_b15` a greedy sweep in place (never walks), `p1_b15`, `p1_b25` one vantage
point, `p3_b25`, `p3_b40` several. (With budget 15 a walk plus its look never fits after the sweep, so `p1_b15`
took exactly the same warm-up as `p0_b15` in all 39 episodes -- which makes the pair a measurement of the noise floor.)

## Reading

1. **No measurable effect on SR or SPL.** Every arm's dSR lies within +-2.6 points (CI about +-10), every dSPL
   within +2.6 points (CIs straddle zero), McNemar p = 1.0 throughout, 2-3 flips each way. The two arms whose
   warm-ups were **identical in all 39 episodes** (`p1_b15` / `p0_b15`) still differ by SR 82.1 vs 84.6, SPL 36.2
   vs 38.0, 3 success flips and 7 episodes with |dSPL| > 0.05: that is the noise floor of the live oracle
   (temperature 0 does not make Ollama deterministic under concurrent requests, LESSONS.md 2026-10-31), and every
   difference between a vantage arm and the spin is of that size or smaller.
2. **The warm-up does what it was built to do, and it does not matter.** At equal action counts the vantage arms
   uncover 2-3 m2 more than the spin (area at action 30: 33-34.5 against 31.6 m2); `p3_b40`, which walked 1.9 m
   and spent 24 actions, ends its warm-up with 35.3 m2 against the spin's 27.7, sees the target within 30 actions
   in 54 % of the episodes against 38 %, and has the earliest median first sight (action 26 against 42). None of
   it moves SR. An episode runs 212 actions on average and the map the search needs is far larger than what any
   first look shows; what decides the episode is the room order the oracle picks and the closing's approach, both
   of which the warm-up perturbs rather than improves.
3. **Per action, the spin is the most efficient look.** 2.3 m2 per warm-up action for the spin and for the greedy
   sweep in place (`p0_b15`, `p1_b15`), 1.6 for one vantage point, 1.2 for `p3_b40`: turning is free of
   translation, and a walk's expected area is discounted by the survival prior and by the actions the walk costs.
   The greedy sweep in place saves about 2 actions on the spin's 10 (it stops when the remaining arc meets walls
   within a metre or two) for +1.6 m2 at action 10, and is the arm with the best SR and SPL -- within the noise.
4. **The flips are divergence, not information.** `Z6MFQCViBuw/000183` flips to success in every arm: the spin
   locked the cabinet at action 3 and spent 380 actions in an occluded approach from that side (two 160-action
   closing bounds, 36 escape actions, DTG 4.5 m at the end); the greedy sweep turned the other way, locked it at
   action 8 from a better side and STOPped at 73. `TbHJrupSAjP/000040` flips to failure in four arms: the spin saw
   the stool at 138 and STOPped at 164 after 9 oracle answers; the vantage arms, with a different map at the first
   loop point, asked 70-77 times and never saw it. `TbHJrupSAjP/000162` flips to success in all five: the spin's
   room order never reached the chest of drawers (59 answers), every other order did. In every case the runs part
   at the first action (the warm-up turns the other way or walks) and the oracle's room order does the rest.

**Conclusion.** On this set the first look is not where the episodes are won or lost: ten turns in place, a greedy
sweep, or a walk to a vantage point give the same SR and SPL to within the oracle's own run-to-run noise, and the
spin is the cheapest per action. The greedy sweep in place (`p0_b15`) is the only variant with no downside -- it
takes 8 actions instead of 10 for the same or a little more map -- and the only one worth keeping as an option; a
walk before the first decision is not worth its actions. The laptop study (`mp3d/warmup_compare.sh`) repeats the
comparison with the 3B model and a shared answer store across all arms, which removes the noise floor measured here;
at n = 39 a true effect would have to exceed about 10 SR points to show, so a null there is also the expected result.
